THE ORDINANCE OF THE YEAR

The year of ḤANOK and YOḆELIM is exactly 364 days — 52 weeks — four equal seasons of 13 weeks. Each season holds three months of 30, 30, and 31 days; the added day of each third month is a day of remembrance — the new moons of the first, fourth, seventh, and tenth months, standing at the four turnings of the sun. The year begins at the spring turning, on the fourth day of the week — the day the lights were set in the expanse — so every appointed time keeps its own weekday forever: the sheaf is always waved on the first day of the week, and the fiftieth day always falls on the first day of the week. Nothing drifts, because the courses do not change.
